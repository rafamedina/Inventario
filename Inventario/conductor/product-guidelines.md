# Product Guidelines

## Language and Prose
- **Concise Spanish**: Use clear, direct Spanish for all technical documentation and end-user guides.
- **Documentation Strategy**: All in-code comments, READMEs, and user-facing messages should be written in professional, concise Spanish.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Odoo Enterprise UI**: Ensure full compatibility with Odoo's native layout and interactions.
- **Interaction Model**: Use standard Odoo 15.0+ widgets, views (list, form, kanban), and navigation patterns to maintain consistency.
- **Data Integrity**: Prioritize clear error messaging and validation at the model level to ensure high data quality.

## Visual Aesthetic
- **Standard Odoo Design**: Maintain the standard Odoo visual look and feel.
- **Theming**: Do not use custom CSS or themes; stick to the standard Odoo Enterprise color palette and components for seamless integration.
